The science bit

So… what is stress?

Stress is your body's reaction to pressure or change. It is a built-in alarm system.

⚡ Short-term (acute)

Comes and goes. Before a test, a match, a speech. Your body handles this well.

🐌 Long-term (chronic)

Stays switched on for weeks. Bad sleep, getting sick more often, feeling drained.

💡 Like blood pressure: everyone has some. It only becomes a problem when it stays too high for too long.

How does stress show up?

🫀 Body

  • Racing heart
  • Headaches
  • Stomach knots / butterflies
  • Tight shoulders, clenched jaw
  • Sweaty hands

🧠 Mind

  • Worrying on repeat
  • Mind racing… or going blank
  • Can't concentrate
  • Can't make decisions

🎭 Behaviour

  • Snapping at people
  • Sleeping badly
  • Avoiding things
  • Endless scrolling

🍲 Cannon fed cats food mixed with bismuth, which shows up on X-rays. He could watch the stomach churn: digestion ON.

The science behind it 📈

Stress is not all bad

😴
Bored & sleepy

Good stress has a name: eustress. It's the buzz before a match, a performance, or your first day somewhere new.

Secret weapon · Say it together
"I'm not nervous.
I'm EXCITED!" 🚀
🤫 Round 1: whisper it
🗣️ Round 2: normal voice

In a 2014 Harvard study, people who said "I am excited" before a performance did better than people who tried to calm down.
